## The business problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For two weeks she wrote down her daily sales and kept a rough record of her stock, and she wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, and what she should restock and focus on. This project uses core Python only, with no pandas or NumPy, to answer those questions and give her specific recommendations.

## How to run

[Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/elmbondo/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb), then choose Runtime, then Run all.

## Top three findings

1. Total sales were KES 46,450 over 14 days, and week 2 (KES 24,400) was 10.7% higher than week 1 (KES 22,050).
2. Weekends average KES 4,900 a day against KES 2,685 on weekdays, which is 82.5% higher.
3. Tomatoes (18 kg) and sukuma (9 kg) are at or below their reorder levels, and avocado has the best gross margin at 42.9%.
